About

A solo maker, under one company name.

Pete De Paola builds small apps and ships them himself. PLJA Corp is the company name he uses for that work. It matches his main X account, @PLJA_AI. The public shop people already know is Analog Arcade.

The idea is simple: local-first where the product can work that way, and a one-time price when a product is sold. House Radar is $2.99 once on the App Store, with no subscription and no in-app purchases. Local Mass Finder and Keysong are free. House Binder is described as free on its live sign-in page. Card Check shows no price on its live page.

Where a product can run without an account, it says so. Keysong and the Local Mass Finder app need no account. House Binder is private to your sign-in. Card Check can be used without an account, but a check is only saved to My Cards if you sign in first.

What this draft does not claim

This site does not claim that PLJA Corp has been filed as a legal entity. That still needs to be confirmed. On the App Store, the seller name today is Peter De Paola, enrolled as an individual. Showing PLJA Corp as the seller would take a later change, and only after a legal entity and the other steps Apple requires are in place.

Pete is the only maker behind these five products.
