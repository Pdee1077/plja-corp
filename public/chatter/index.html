  1. Tuesday split in two. Chips fell hard (the SMH chip fund −7.3%) while the S&P 500 fell only 0.5%, the Russell 2000 rose 0.6%, and staples (XLP) rose 1.7%.
  2. Chatter spiked on a few names, and most of those had news. Dell's Reddit mentions rose 6.8× in a day (163 vs 24) with the stock down. Ambarella and Walmart also jumped. Nvidia's talk went the other way: 681 mentions vs 1,883 the day before (0.4×).

Talk vs normal is StockTwits talk against its own 20-day normal when we have it, otherwise Reddit mentions against the day before. NO NEWS means abnormal talk with two or fewer headlines counted for that ticker in the last 24 hours. Talk only means the talk jumped and the price is quiet.
