Privacy

Privacy on this website.

This page covers the PLJA Corp company website only — the pages you are reading now. It does not replace the privacy page of each product.

What this site collects

This website is a static site. It does not use accounts, cookies, or analytics. It does not run advertising. It does not have a contact form that sends information to our servers. If you open a mailto link on your own device, that uses your own email app.

We do not collect personal information through this website itself. Ordinary web logs may be kept by the host (Cloudflare) when a page is requested, as they are for most sites. This draft does not add any extra tracking on top of that.
